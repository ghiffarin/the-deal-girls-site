In August 2019, Ayesha founded Gores Denai Foundation, an impact-oriented organization in Indonesia that helped 1,000+ Indonesian public school students access international opportunities, from MUN competitions to Ivy League prep.

The Deal Girls is her second act: not fixing access for students, but opening the rooms that women building companies and careers deserve to be in.

02 — In her words

It all started in 2019.

Watch the full story on Instagram →

At 15, I started my first nonprofit because I kept noticing the same thing: talented public school students in Indonesia just never had a reason to believe the world outside was reachable.

That's where I first learned talent was never the rare thing. Access was.

Years later, I stepped into my first VC internship: scouting women founders, sitting 1:1 with them, listening to how they thought. Bold, occasionally unhinged in the best way, endlessly ambitious. And I was hooked.

But I also noticed how exclusive this world can be. The right school. The right circle. These things moved faster than talent did.

I guess my thing was never really VC or nonprofits specifically. It's always been about giving people from underrepresented backgrounds a way into rooms that only make space for the same schools, same circles.

That's what I'm building. That's what The Deal Girls is.

09 — Questions

Good questions.

Who is TDG for?+

Ambitious women in or entering the startup and VC world — whether you're building a company, working at a startup, exploring investing, or still figuring out where you fit. If you're serious about the space and not finding the right rooms, this is for you.

Is it free to join?+

Yes, TDG membership is completely free. We're focused on building real proof before we charge anything.

What exactly is TDG Intro?+

Our flagship mentorship matchmaking program. You apply, tell us where you are and where you want to go, and we match you with a mentor a few steps ahead, from VC firms, banks, and startup ecosystems across SEA and Europe. Less like a course, more like being introduced to the right person at the right time.

Do I need to be based in SEA or Europe?+

Not at all! The Deal Girls is open to women wherever they are. For now our regional focus is SEA ⇄ Europe. We have women in Jakarta, Manila, Rotterdam, and beyond.

What do I get when I join?+

Three things: a curated match through TDG Intro, in-person events across Rotterdam and Southeast Asia, and a bi-weekly newsletter with curated opportunities — grants, funding, jobs, and programs for women in startups and VC. No noise, no generic content. Just the people and opportunities worth your time.
