01 — Why partner with The Deal Girls

Most women's networks reach a lot of people and know very few of them. We do it the other way round.

We know them

Everyone in our community is a founder, an operator or an investor. We know who they are and where they're headed, and we introduce them to the right people.

Warm, not cold

When you partner with us, you meet them through a warm introduction, and not a cold ad.

Two ecosystems

And because we sit between Southeast Asia and Europe, you reach talent and ideas from two ecosystems that rarely meet.
