About The Deal Girls

Get closer to the world of startups and venture capital.

The Deal Girls is a media and community platform for women curious about building a business, working at a startup, or backing founders. Through stories, practical guides, and events, we make it easier to understand how the entrepreneurship world works and meet other women figuring out where they want to go in it.

We're based in the Netherlands, with roots in Southeast Asia and a growing community across both regions.

01 — Meet the team

The people behind it.

founder.jpg

Ayesha Felice

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

I wanted more women to get an early look at this world.

I got my first real look at startups as a venture analyst intern. I spent my time looking for promising founders and learning how investors decide which ideas to back. I remember thinking how much bigger the world of startups felt once I could see how it worked up close.

I'd already spent years building Gores Denai, helping Indonesian public-school students discover opportunities they might otherwise never hear about. I found myself asking a similar question about startups and investing. How many women would be curious about this world if they got to see more of it?

That question became The Deal Girls. I wanted a place where you could ask what a venture capitalist actually does, hear how someone got her first customer, or meet another woman who was also thinking, Could I build something of my own?

Karola Szatmári-Philips

Chief Operating Officer

It's easier to picture a different path when you've seen someone take it.

Growing up in Romania, I saw talented people follow a familiar route. Finish school, choose a respected career, and stick with it. Starting something of your own felt like a bigger leap, especially for women, because there were so few examples of what that path could look like.

Moving to the Netherlands and leading ASIF Ventures changed what I could picture for myself. I met young founders who took their ideas seriously enough to try them. I also noticed how few women were in venture and how often the women I met had found their way in through a chance encounter.

That's what drew me to The Deal Girls. I want more women to see these paths early enough to imagine taking them themselves, long before their first pitch, application, or job.

03 — How we got here

How we got here.

  1. 2023

    The idea took shape.

    Ayesha started sketching out a platform for women curious about startups and venture capital, and brainstorming what to call it.

  2. April 2025

    Our first post went live.

    The Deal Girls launches on Instagram.

  3. August 2025

    Maudy Ayunda backed the idea.

    The Deal Girls receives funding from the Indonesian actress and entrepreneur.

  4. December 2025

    We announced our first bootcamp.

    We took The Deal Girls from the Instagram feed into a program where members can learn together.

  5. July 2026

    We became a registered business.

    The Deal Girls officially registered in the Netherlands.

  6. August 2026

    We hosted our first event in the Netherlands.

    Our community came together in person for the first time in the country.

  7. November 2026

    Karola joined as Chief Operating Officer.

    Ayesha and Karola began working together to build the next chapter of The Deal Girls.
